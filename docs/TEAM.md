## Development Philosophy

The Nova project values simplicity, stability, and clarity over trends and premature optimization. Our goal is to build a dependable foundation first, then add power and polish deliberately.

### Principles
- **Simple first, powerful later.**
- **No unnecessary frameworks or build tools.**
- **No hot reload.** Developers restart manually after each change.
- **Optimization and bundling will come only after Nova is functionally mature.**
- **Every feature or dependency must justify its inclusion with a clear, immediate benefit.**
