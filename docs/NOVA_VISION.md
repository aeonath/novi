# Nova Vision Document  
**Revised October 2025**  
**Project Type:** AI Orchestration Tool (AOT)**  
**Studio:** MiraNova Studios  

---

## Overview  
Nova is no longer envisioned as a traditional AI-powered IDE. Instead, it is being redefined as an **AI Orchestration Tool (AOT)** — a structured environment designed to help creators, developers, and teams collaborate effectively with AI agents such as ChatGPT, Claude, or others.  

Nova serves as the *mission control center* for your AI-driven development process, providing a clear workflow for planning, instructing, and evaluating AI work through organized documents, sprint management, and markdown-based project structures.

---

## Purpose  
The purpose of Nova is to simplify the process of working *with* AI — not just through prompts, but through structured collaboration. It helps users design, track, and verify iterative AI work in a consistent and reproducible way.  

Nova empowers users to:  
- **Create structured sprints** (`SPRINT1.md`, `SPRINT1_TASK1.md`, etc.) that AI agents can interpret and execute.  
- **Generate and review AI outputs** such as `SPRINT1_TASK1_SUMMARY.md` and change logs.  
- **Maintain configuration control** through a `CLAUDE_CONFIG`, `GPT_CONFIG`, or other agent-specific files.  
- **Coordinate multiple AI agents** working across tasks, projects, and languages.  
- **Use a markdown-based editor** optimized for sprint management, AI task review, and version documentation.  

---

## Philosophy  
Nova follows MiraNova Studios’ guiding principle:  
> **Build. Learn. Iterate.**

Rather than directly writing code or managing an editor’s runtime, Nova focuses on enabling *process discipline* for AI collaboration. By formalizing how we communicate with AI systems, Nova bridges the gap between human creativity and machine execution — allowing users to develop like a team, even when their team is AI.

---

## Functionality  
Nova’s initial implementation will include:  

- 🗂️ **Project Directory Management**  
  Organize AI-related documents, sprints, and configurations within a dedicated `nova` directory.  

- ✍️ **Markdown Editor**  
  A focused editing environment tailored for writing and reviewing AI-oriented documents (tasks, summaries, plans, etc.).  

- 🧠 **Sprint Creation Wizard**  
  Step-by-step tools to create new sprints, define tasks, and generate the necessary file scaffolding.  

- ⚙️ **Configuration Tools**  
  Simple interfaces for editing `CLAUDE_CONFIG`, `GPT_CONFIG`, or future agent configuration files.  

- 📈 **Iteration Tracking**  
  Allow users to review what each AI agent has produced for a given sprint, ensuring accountability and clarity.  

---

## Long-Term Vision  
While Nova will initially operate as an offline orchestration environment, future versions may incorporate direct integration with AI APIs, allowing in-app task execution and reporting.  

When the time is right, Nova may evolve into a hybrid IDE + AOT system — combining structured AI workflow management with live model interactions.  

For now, the goal is clear:  
> **Nova helps you orchestrate intelligence — not replace it.**

---

## Naming Note  
The acronym **AOT (AI Orchestration Tool)** is intentionally symbolic. It aligns with the creative spirit of MiraNova and coincidentally echoes the first three letters of *Aotau*, the musical project also created by MiraNova’s founder. This reinforces the shared theme across all MiraNova endeavors: harmony between creativity, structure, and intelligence.

---

## Summary  
Nova is:  
- A tool for **AI collaboration**, not direct code execution.  
- A system for **structured iteration**, not freeform prompting.  
- A bridge between **human vision** and **machine capability**.  

With Nova, AI becomes a team member — guided by process, purpose, and clarity.
